01 / ABOUT THE RESEARCHER

Connecting physics
to prediction.

I am a computational materials scientist developing models that connect processing conditions, thermodynamics, and microstructural evolution.

I successfully defended my PhD at the Silesian University of Technology, Poland, on September 8, 2026. My doctoral research integrated phase-field modeling, thermodynamic calculations, tensor methods, multiphysics simulations, and machine learning to build computational foundations for digital-twin approaches.

In January 2027, I will join McMaster University, Canada, as a postdoctoral researcher supported by the Canada Impact+ Award. My upcoming work will focus broadly on modeling materials in the extreme environments relevant to Nuclear Fusion.
